Connections

USB-C carries power and USB 2.0 data. The nRF52840 adds Bluetooth Low Energy for wireless control and communication.

Fifteen GPIO breakouts expose the controller for sensors, controls, and small peripherals. Dedicated SWD access supports debugging, alongside an onboard LED and reset access.
